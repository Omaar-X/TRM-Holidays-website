Cancellation Policy

HomeCancellation Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Plans change, and we will always try to help. The cancellation and refund terms depend on the service you booked and on the rules of the airline, hotel or operator behind it, so we confirm them with you in writing before you pay any advance.

How to cancel or change a booking

Tell us as early as you can by phone, WhatsApp or email, using the contact details at the bottom of this page. Please include your name, the booking or package name and your travel date. A cancellation counts from the time we receive your message.

Air tickets

  • Refunds, date changes and name corrections follow the fare rules of the airline you booked.
  • Many discounted fares are non-refundable or carry a change fee. We will tell you which type applies when we quote the fare.
  • Airline refunds can take time to reach us, and we pass them on to you once received, less any airline charges.

Hotel bookings

  • Each hotel sets its own free-cancellation window and no-show charges. We will share them with you at the time of booking.
  • Non-refundable and promotional rates cannot be refunded once confirmed.

Tour packages

  • The advance you pay is used to reserve hotels, transport and services for your group, so part of it may not be refundable once those are booked.
  • The cancellation terms for your package, including how much is refundable at different times before departure, are confirmed to you in writing before you pay.
  • Peak-season and festival-period packages may have stricter terms.

Visa assistance

Embassy and visa fees are paid to the issuing authority and are generally not refundable once an application has been submitted, whether or not the visa is approved. Our service fee is explained when you apply.

If we have to change or cancel

If a supplier cancels or we have to make a significant change to your booking, we will contact you promptly and agree an alternative or a refund with you.

Refunds

Approved refunds are returned using the same method you paid with, after we have received the corresponding amount from the airline, hotel or operator. We will let you know the expected timing when your cancellation is confirmed.
