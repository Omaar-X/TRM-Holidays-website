Our story

Your Memory Maker, at Home and Abroad

TRM Holidays was founded in 2023 in Banani, Dhaka, with a simple idea: planning a trip should feel as exciting as taking one. What began as a small team booking tickets for friends and families has grown into a full-service travel agency trusted by travellers across Bangladesh.

Today we handle the whole journey in one place — air tickets, hotel bookings, visa processing and complete tour packages. Whether it's a weekend at Cox's Bazar or Sajek, or a family holiday in Dubai, Thailand or the Maldives, one team plans it with you and stays reachable on WhatsApp before you book and while you're away.

Every package is priced up front, every hotel is one we have checked, and every trip has a real person behind it who answers when you call. That is what "Your Memory Maker" means to us.
