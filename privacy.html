Privacy Policy

HomePrivacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

TRM Holidays (“we”, “us”) is a travel agency based in Banani, Dhaka. This page explains what personal information this website collects, why, and what we do with it.

What we collect

We only collect what you type into our forms or send us directly:

  • Contact form: your name, phone number, email address, the subject and tour you are asking about, and your message.
  • Tour booking inquiry: your name, phone number, email address, preferred travel date, number of travellers and the package you selected.
  • WhatsApp, phone and email: whatever you choose to share when you contact us outside the website.

Flight and hotel searches on this site are not saved. We do not ask for card numbers, bank details or passwords on this website.

How we use it

  • To reply to your question and prepare a quote.
  • To arrange and confirm bookings you ask us to make, including passing the details needed to airlines, hotels, tour operators and visa offices.
  • To contact you about the trip you enquired about.

We do not sell your personal information.

Where it is stored

Form submissions are received through Google Apps Script and stored in a Google Sheet managed by TRM Holidays and used only by our staff. Google processes this data on our behalf under its own terms and privacy policy.

Third-party services

To load, this website uses services provided by other companies, including Google Fonts and Google Maps, content delivery networks for icons and scripts, and Unsplash for photographs. When your browser loads these, the provider may receive your IP address and basic browser details. We do not control how they handle that data.

Cookies and local storage

The public pages of this site do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Sharing your information

We share your details only with the suppliers needed to deliver the service you asked for (for example an airline, hotel or visa office), or where the law requires us to.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to tell you what information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. Contact us using the details below and we will respond as soon as we reasonably can.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle personal information we will update this page and the date at the top.
